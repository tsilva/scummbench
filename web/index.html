About these results

Each line is one run. A step marks the action count at which a milestone was first verified. Reaching 100% farther left means fewer actions to finish.

Dates identify newer and older runs. The horizontal axis measures actions, not calendar time. Individual runs show examples, not a statistical claim of improvement.

Only runs from the selected game and benchmark version appear together. Each version fixes the starting conditions, progress rubric, completion rule and action definition.

Actions count submitted records under that version’s rules. Batches are charged at submission; milestone locations do not imply exact low-level input timing.

Live results come from finished ScummBench runs and registered archives. Missing or inconsistent evidence is reported here rather than turned into a chart. Sample data is entirely illustrative. Current runs are development evidence, not independent attestation or official scores.
