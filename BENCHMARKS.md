# Benchmarks

ScummBench owns new AgentSCUMM playthrough result registration.
[Historical AgentSCUMM records](https://github.com/tsilva/agentscumm/blob/main/BENCHMARKS.md)
remain in their original repository.

Registered playthroughs disclose walkthrough assistance, provenance, usage and
verification limits. They are development evidence, not controlled success-rate
estimates. Fixed-task runner results remain separate from interactive play.
See [evaluation and registration](docs/evaluation.md#registered-playthroughs).
